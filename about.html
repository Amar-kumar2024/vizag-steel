About Vizag Steel

History, Leadership & Company Information

Vizag Steel, officially known as Visakhapatnam Steel Plant (VSP), is the corporate entity of Rashtriya Ispat Nigam Limited (RINL). RINL is a Navratna Public Sector Enterprise under the Ministry of Steel, Government of India. The Visakhapatnam Steel Plant is known as the country's first shore-based integrated steel plant.

History of Vizag Steel

The idea of establishing an integrated steel plant at Visakhapatnam emerged from the Government of India's objective of promoting industrial development and meeting the aspirations of the people of Andhra Pradesh.

1970
The Government of India announced the decision to establish an integrated public-sector steel plant at Visakhapatnam.
20 January 1971
The foundation stone of the Visakhapatnam Steel Plant was laid by the then Prime Minister of India, Smt. Indira Gandhi.
1979
India and the erstwhile Soviet Union signed an agreement for setting up the integrated steel plant.
1982
Construction of the plant started on 1 February 1982. On 18 February 1982, the Government of India formed Rashtriya Ispat Nigam Limited (RINL).
1989–1990
The plant achieved its first casting of hot metal into pig iron, marking an important milestone in its development.
1992
The original integrated plant was commissioned and began its journey as a major steel producer.

Who Founded Vizag Steel?

Vizag Steel does not have a single individual founder in the conventional private-company sense.

According to RINL's official history, the sustained campaign led by Shri Tenneti Viswanadham played an important role in the establishment of the Visakhapatnam Steel Plant.

The Government of India subsequently took the decision to establish the integrated steel plant, while Smt. Indira Gandhi, the then Prime Minister of India, laid its foundation stone on 20 January 1971.

A Collective National Project

The development of Vizag Steel was therefore a major Government of India project involving political leadership, public institutions, Indian engineers and workers, and technical cooperation with the erstwhile Soviet Union.

Who Runs Vizag Steel?

Visakhapatnam Steel Plant operates under Rashtriya Ispat Nigam Limited (RINL), a Navratna Public Sector Enterprise under the administrative control of the Ministry of Steel, Government of India.

Shri MNVS Prabhakar
Chairman-cum-Managing Director (CMD), RINL

Shri MNVS Prabhakar assumed charge as Chairman-cum-Managing Director of RINL in May 2026.

He is an experienced steel-sector professional with more than three decades of experience in the Indian steel industry.

The CMD is the chief executive of the company and is responsible for the efficient functioning of RINL, subject to the company's Board of Directors and the Government of India.

What Does Vizag Steel Produce?

RINL's Visakhapatnam Steel Plant produces a range of steel and iron products for industrial, infrastructure, construction and engineering applications.

  • TMT Rebars
  • Wire Rods
  • Billets
  • Rounds
  • Squares
  • Angles
  • Channels
  • Pig Iron
  • Other long steel products

Why Vizag Steel is Important

Visakhapatnam Steel Plant is significant to India's steel industry because it is the country's first shore-based integrated steel plant.

The plant serves customers across various industrial sectors and has a nationwide marketing and distribution network.

RINL is also recognized as a Navratna Public Sector Enterprise under the Ministry of Steel, Government of India.
