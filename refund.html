Refund Policy

Last Updated: 10 August 2026

This Refund Policy explains the terms applicable to payments and refund requests made through the Vizag Steel Price-List Website. Please read this policy carefully before placing an order or making a payment.

1. General Refund Policy

Payments made through the Website are generally treated as final once the transaction has been successfully processed.

A refund is not automatically available merely because a customer changes their mind after making a successful payment.

Any refund request will be reviewed according to the circumstances of the transaction and the applicable terms of the order.

2. Eligible Refund Situations

A refund may be considered in circumstances such as:

  • Duplicate payment for the same order.
  • Payment successfully deducted from the customer's account but the order was not successfully created due to a technical or transaction error.
  • An order being cancelled by us before fulfilment where a refund is applicable.
  • Any other situation where a refund is specifically approved by us under the applicable order terms.

Approval of a refund request is subject to verification of the transaction and the applicable terms.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds may not be provided in situations including, but not limited to:

  • Change of mind after an order has been confirmed or processed.
  • Incorrect information provided by the customer.
  • Failure to provide an accurate delivery address or contact information.
  • Orders that have already been dispatched or delivered, except where otherwise required by applicable law or agreed order terms.
  • Delays or failures caused by banks, payment gateways, card networks, UPI services, or other third-party payment providers.

4. Duplicate Payments

If the same order is accidentally paid for more than once, the customer should contact us as soon as possible.

After verification, an eligible duplicate payment may be refunded through the original payment method or another appropriate method.

5. Failed Payment but Amount Deducted

In some cases, a payment may appear to fail even though the amount has been deducted from the customer's bank account or payment instrument.

Customers should first allow the applicable bank or payment service provider to complete its transaction reconciliation process.

If the amount is not automatically reversed or the order remains unsuccessful, the customer may contact us with the relevant transaction details for review.

6. Refund Request Process

To request a refund, customers should contact us with sufficient transaction information, such as:

  • Customer name
  • Order number
  • Transaction ID
  • Payment date
  • Amount paid
  • Reason for requesting the refund

We may request additional information where necessary to verify the transaction.

7. Refund Processing

Once a refund has been approved, it will generally be initiated through the original payment method or the applicable payment service provider.

Important: The time required for the refunded amount to appear in the customer's bank account, card, UPI account, or other payment instrument may depend on the payment gateway, bank, card issuer, or financial institution.

We are not responsible for delays caused by third-party financial institutions after the refund has been successfully initiated.

8. Cancellation and Refund

Order cancellation and refund eligibility may depend on the stage at which the cancellation request is received.

Once an order has been dispatched, delivered, or otherwise processed, cancellation or refund may not be possible unless specifically approved or required under applicable law.

9. Payment Gateway Issues

Payments may be processed through third-party payment gateways or financial service providers.

We are not responsible for technical failures, authorization failures, bank downtime, UPI issues, card-network failures, or delays caused by third-party payment providers.

However, customers may contact us so that we can assist in reviewing the transaction status.

10. Fraudulent or Unauthorized Transactions

If you believe that an unauthorized or fraudulent transaction has been made using your payment method, you should immediately contact your bank or payment service provider.

You may also contact us with the transaction details so that we can review the transaction from our side.

11. Changes to This Refund Policy

We may update or modify this Refund Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, payment processes, legal requirements, or business practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding payments, refunds, cancellations, or transaction-related issues, please contact us using the details provided on the Website.
